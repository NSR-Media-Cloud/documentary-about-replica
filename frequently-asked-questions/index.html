FAQ

RTA’s Frequently Asked Questions

  1. The Reel Truth Academy is an online film school for people who want to make honest nonfiction. Every course is taught by working filmmakers, with short practical lessons you can watch at your own pace, a private community for feedback, and live sessions with guests from across the industry.

  2. Anyone with a story to tell. Our students range from first-time filmmakers with a phone and an idea to editors, producers and journalists who want to move into documentary. There are no entry requirements, and each course tells you up front what experience it assumes.

  3. Accounts are personal and cannot be shared. If you would like to learn together, our team plans give each person their own login at a reduced rate.

  4. Yes. Courses are self-paced, so you can enrol in several and move between them. Most students find it easier to focus on one course at a time and add the next once their first project is underway.

  5. Access never expires. Once you enrol you keep your lessons, downloads and community membership for as long as the academy runs, including any updates we make to the course.

  6. On any modern browser, on your phone or tablet, or on a connected TV. Your progress follows you from device to device.

  7. After you enrol we email you a link to set your password. Use that email address on the login page. If the message does not arrive within a few minutes, check your spam folder or contact our support team.

  8. Each course is split into chapters of short video lessons with exercises and downloadable worksheets. Live teaching happens in our monthly sessions, where guests and instructors answer questions in real time. Every session is recorded for those who cannot attend.

  9. Once a month we invite a working producer, editor, cinematographer or commissioner to talk through their process and take your questions. Dates are announced in the portal at least two weeks ahead.

  10. A camera you can control, even a recent phone, and a way to record clean sound. Our gear guides cover what to buy and what to borrow at every budget, and the lessons teach technique rather than specific brands.

  11. Worksheets, templates and transcripts can be downloaded. Video lessons are streamed to protect our instructors’ work, but they are optimised to play smoothly on slower connections.

  12. Because courses are delivered digitally, we do not offer refunds once you have access to the lessons. Before you buy you can read the full chapter outline and watch preview lessons, and our team is happy to answer any questions first.

  13. Yes, both. Payment plans split the cost over several months at no extra charge, and we award a small number of scholarships each season. Details and application dates are on the funding page.

  14. Every course ends in a practical project, and most chapters include a short exercise. Assignments are optional, but students who complete them get written notes from instructors and peers.

  15. Finish every chapter and submit your final project, and the certificate is issued automatically. You can download it as a PDF or share a verification link with employers and festivals.

  16. Yes. Teams of five or more receive volume pricing, shared progress reports and an onboarding call. Send us a note through the enterprise page and we will put together a quote.

  17. A membership covers the course library, the student community, the monthly guest sessions, the template library and entry to our seasonal screenings. Individual courses can also be bought on their own.

  18. Email our support team with your name and the address you enrolled with, and describe what you were trying to do. A screenshot helps. We reply within one working day.

  19. Send it to us through the contact page and a member of the team will reply personally. If it is something many students ask, we will add it to this list.
