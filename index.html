What’s included
when you join RTA

  1. Work through a full library of short, practical lessons, each one built around a real film, so you always know what to do next whether you are planning, shooting or editing.

  2. Every lesson, worksheet and template is available whenever you want it, on any device. Pause for a month, come back, and pick up exactly where you left off with nothing expiring.

  3. Once a month a working producer, editor or cinematographer joins a live session and takes your questions. Every call is recorded, so you can catch up if the time does not suit you.

  4. A private group of makers at every level, where you can share a rough cut, ask for a second pair of eyes, find a collaborator or simply see how other people solve the same problems.

  5. Short practical assignments turn what you watch into footage you can use. Finish a course and its final project and you receive a certificate that you can share with anyone who asks.

  6. Send us a work in progress and get written notes from a working filmmaker, so the changes you make next are the ones that matter most to your film.
