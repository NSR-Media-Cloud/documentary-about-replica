Overview

Entry Rules & Requirements

Enter your film

  • 24 Hours

    Film everything within one 24-hour window. A short pick-up session of up to two hours on another day is allowed.

  • RTA Members

    Open to current RTA members, entering as the director.

  • Duration

    Runtime must be 2 to 6 minutes long.

  • Foreign Languages

    Non-English films need English subtitles burned in.

  • Fresh Work

    Only films made for this contest are eligible.

  • Archival Footage

    Archive material is fine when it comes from your subject and is relevant to their story. List every source in your submission notes.

  • Stock Footage

    No stock footage is accepted.

  • Submission

    Upload your finished film to Vimeo or YouTube and paste the link into the entry form before the closing date. Keep the video unlisted if you like, but do not make it private, because the judges need to be able to watch it. Late entries cannot be accepted, and you must hold the rights to both the film and every piece of music in it. No exceptions will be made. Ask us early if anything is unclear.
