Privacy Policy

This policy explains how The Reel Truth Academy (“we”, “us”) collects, uses and shares information about you when you visit our website, take one of our courses or contact our team. It also describes the choices you have. By using the site you acknowledge the practices described here.

We try to collect only what we need to teach you well, keep your account safe and run the academy responsibly. We do not sell your personal information.

This policy applies to information collected through the website, the student portal, our email and text messages and our live sessions. It does not apply to information collected by other organisations that you reach through links on our site.

If you have questions about this policy, the final section explains how to reach us.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or the law. When we make a material change we will update the date at the top of the page and, where appropriate, tell you by email or through the student portal.

How we collect and use your personal information

We collect information in three ways: directly from you when you create an account, enrol or write to us; automatically through your browser and device when you use the site; and from partners such as payment providers who confirm that a purchase went through.

The sections below list what we collect in each case and what we do with it.

Where the law requires a legal basis for using your information, we rely on one of four: performing our contract with you, our legitimate interests in running and improving the academy, your consent, or a legal obligation.

Personal information we collect

Depending on how you use the academy, we may collect:

  • Contact details: your name, email address, phone number and country.
  • Account details: your login, course progress, assignments and community posts.
  • Purchase details: the courses you bought, the amount paid and the last four digits of your card. Full card numbers are handled by our payment provider and never reach us.
  • Device details: your browser, operating system, approximate location from your IP address and the pages you visit.
  • Messages: anything you send to our support team or submit through a form.
  • Preferences: whether you have opted in to email, text messages or analytics cookies.
  • Profile details: a photo, biography or links that you choose to add to your student profile.
  • Survey answers: your replies to questionnaires, competitions or applications for funding.

Some of this information is required to provide the service; where that is the case we tell you at the point of collection. If you choose not to provide it, we may not be able to give you access to the course.

  • Usage records: which lessons you watch, for how long and when you return, so that we can save your place and show your progress.
  • Payment status: whether a payment succeeded, failed or was refunded.
  • Support history: the messages and files exchanged with our team and our notes on how a problem was solved.
  • Event details: your registration and attendance at live sessions and screenings.

How we use your personal information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Create and manage your account and give you access to the courses you bought.
  • Process payments, issue receipts and prevent fraud.
  • Send service messages such as password resets, session reminders and certificates.
  • Send news and offers if you have opted in, and let you opt out at any time.
  • Reply to your questions and give technical support.
  • Understand how the site is used so we can fix problems and improve the lessons.
  • Keep the community safe and enforce our terms of use.
  • Meet our legal and accounting obligations.
  • Verify certificates of completion when an employer or festival asks us to.
  • Run competitions, screenings and funding applications you enter.
  • Test new features and make sure they work on the devices our students use.
  • Produce summary statistics about the academy that do not identify any individual.

We combine information from different sources only where it helps us deliver one of the purposes above, for example matching a purchase to your account so that your course unlocks. We do not use your information to make decisions that have a legal or similarly significant effect on you without a person being involved.

Where we send you marketing, every message carries an unsubscribe link, and we honour requests to stop within a few days.

For example, we review anonymous viewing patterns to find lessons that confuse people so that we can improve them, and we look for unusual sign-in activity to protect accounts from being taken over. We keep invoices and payment records for as long as tax and accounting rules require. If a purchase is made as a gift or for a team, we use the recipient’s contact details only to deliver the access that was bought.

We never use the content of your private messages to us for marketing, and we do not use your project work for promotion without asking you first.

Cookies

We use cookies and similar technologies to keep you signed in, remember your preferences and measure how the site performs. Essential cookies are always on because the site cannot work without them. Analytics cookies are optional and you can switch them off from the cookie notice at any time. You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings, although parts of the site may then stop working.

Analytics cookies tell us which pages are visited, how long people stay and where they come from, in a form that does not identify you. We load them only after you accept them in the cookie notice, and you can withdraw consent by clearing the site’s cookies and choosing again.

You can review your choice at any time by clearing the site’s stored data in your browser, after which the cookie notice will appear again.

Some of our providers, for example the video player and the scheduling tool, may set their own cookies when you use them. Those cookies are governed by the provider’s policy, and blocking them may stop the feature from working.

How we disclose personal information

We share personal information only where it is needed to run the academy:

  • Service providers that host our site, process payments, send email and text messages, and provide video streaming and analytics. They may use your information only to work for us.
  • Instructors and community moderators, who see the name and posts you share in course discussions.
  • Professional advisers such as lawyers and accountants, where reasonably necessary.
  • Authorities, where we are required to do so by law or to protect the rights and safety of our students.
  • A successor organisation, if the academy is ever merged with or acquired by another.

Where we share information with a provider, we require them by contract to protect it and to use it only for the purpose we gave them. We may also share information in an aggregated or anonymised form that cannot reasonably be used to identify you.

If we are asked by a public authority to share information, we check that the request is lawful and share only what we must.

Our payment provider handles card details under its own privacy policy, which we encourage you to read. We also publish the names of competition winners with their permission.

User generated content

Posts, comments and project work you share in the student community can be seen by other students and instructors. Please think before you share anything you would not want others to read, and ask us if you would like something removed.

When you delete a post we remove it from public view promptly, although copies may remain in backups for a short period.

Third-party websites and links

Our site may link to other websites whose privacy practices we do not control. This policy does not apply to them, and we encourage you to read the policy of any site you visit.

A link from our site is not an endorsement of the site it leads to or of its owner’s practices.

Children’s data

The academy is not directed to children under 16 and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child has given us information, please contact us and we will delete it.

Students under 18 should ask a parent or guardian before they enrol or send us any personal information.

If we learn that we have collected information from a child without the consent the law requires, we take steps to delete it as soon as we can.

Security and retention of your information

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information, including encryption in transit and access controls. No system is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. We keep personal information for as long as your account is active and for as long afterwards as we need it for legal, tax or dispute purposes, then delete or anonymise it.

Please keep your password secret and tell us at once if you think your account has been accessed without your permission.

When information is no longer needed we delete it or remove anything that could identify you. Backups are overwritten on a regular cycle, so deleted information disappears from them within a few weeks.

Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • Ask for a copy of the personal information we hold about you.
  • Ask us to correct information that is wrong or incomplete.
  • Ask us to delete your information, subject to legal exceptions.
  • Object to or restrict certain uses of your information.
  • Receive your information in a portable format.
  • Withdraw consent at any time where we rely on it, for example for marketing messages.

To use any of these rights, contact us through the contact page. We may need to verify your identity first, and we will reply within the time the law allows.

You are also free to decline optional cookies and marketing messages at any time without affecting how we treat you. We will not penalise you for exercising any of your rights.

If you ask us to delete your information we will remove it from our active systems, and we will tell our providers to do the same where they hold it for us.

You can exercise these choices yourself in many cases: profile details and email preferences can be changed from your account page, and every marketing email contains an unsubscribe link. If you would like help, our support team can carry out the change for you once your identity has been confirmed.

If you are a resident of a place that gives you additional rights, such as the right to know the categories of information we have shared, you may ask us for that too and we will respond in line with the law that applies to you.

Complaints

If you are unhappy with how we handled your information, please tell us first so we can put it right. You may also complain to the data protection authority where you live.

We take every complaint seriously and will reply with the outcome of our review.

International users

Our providers may process information in countries other than your own. Where we transfer information across borders we use safeguards that the law recognises, such as standard contractual clauses.

If you are visiting from outside the country where our servers are located, your information will be transferred to and stored there.

By using the site you understand that your information may be handled under laws that differ from those where you live.

Contact

For any question about this policy or about your information, write to us through the contact page of this website and mark your message “Privacy”.

We will answer your request as quickly as we can.
