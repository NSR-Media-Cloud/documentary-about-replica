Terms of Use

At The Reel Truth Academy we aim to be clear about what you are buying before you buy it. This section explains our approach to refunds, how we support you after a purchase and the few cases where we make an exception.

  1. Overview: Our courses are delivered digitally and every chapter outline is published before purchase, so completed purchases are not refundable.
  2. Course previews: Each course page lists its chapters and includes preview lessons, so you can judge the teaching style and the level before you commit.
  3. Technical support: If a lesson will not load or a download fails, our support team will work with you until the problem is fixed.
  4. Exceptional circumstances: Where a fault on our side keeps you from using a course you paid for, we will extend your access or, if that is not possible, refund you.
  5. Contacting support: Write to us through the contact page and include the email address you enrolled with.

If you are unsure whether a course is right for you, please talk to us before you enrol. We are glad to suggest a better starting point, point you to a free preview lesson or explain how a course fits with the others in the library, and we would much rather answer a question than disappoint you after checkout.

Refund decisions are made by our support team within five working days of your request and are final once confirmed in writing. Nothing in this policy limits any right you have under the consumer laws where you live; if those laws entitle you to a remedy that is not mentioned here, we will honour it.

Where we agree to a refund or an extension, we confirm the details in writing so that both of us have a clear record. Please keep the confirmation email you receive when you enrol, because it is the quickest way for us to find your order.
