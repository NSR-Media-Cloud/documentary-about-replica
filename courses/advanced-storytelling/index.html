Maya Okoro
12 May 2024

Hi all! I wanted to share a small milestone. Six months ago I had a phone, a notebook full of ideas and no clue how to turn them into a film. This weekend my short about my grandmother’s corner shop had its first public screening, and the room went quiet in exactly the place I hoped it would. Thank you to everyone here for the notes on my rough cut, especially the reminder to trust the silences. If you are sitting on an idea and waiting to feel ready, start shooting this week and post the first scene here. We will all help you shape it. Truly, thank you for being such a generous crowd.
